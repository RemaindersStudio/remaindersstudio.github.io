The House

In final polish. Free, always.

A 2D escape room, set in a mansion inspired by the Winchester Mystery House: a building famous for staircases that lead nowhere and doors that open onto walls.

You wake in the bedroom. Everything in it is a clue to something else. Read what you find, look closely at what you're given, and work out which of the things in front of you is worth trusting.

Episode 1 is a single room built to be finished in one sitting. More rooms follow as they are made.

Made in Unity. Plays in your browser, no download.

Playable build coming end of October 2026

The studio

Remainders is a one-person indie studio, founded in 2026. It makes atmospheric, story-driven games rooted in real historical events and places.

Every project starts with research. We keep what the record actually says, and leave alone what it can't explain. If nobody ever found out what happened, the game won't pretend to.

The House is a learning project, built to take a game through the whole process, from first idea to a public release. The larger projects below come after.

Devlog

October 2026

One room, finished

The bedroom now plays from the first click to the way out. Every puzzle depends on the one before it, so a player who skips a step simply won't find what they need.

The hardest part wasn't the puzzles. It was click handling: an object behind a closeup would catch clicks meant for the closeup, and the wardrobe kept closing on top of the player. Closeups now block everything behind them.

Next is the art pass, so that every object in the room can be recognised at a glance, followed by playtests with people who haven't seen it. I'll write up what they get stuck on.

April 2026

Day one

Work started on the bedroom: the layout, the colours, and the feeling that the house has been left alone for a long time.

This is a learning project. The aim is to finish something and release it, not to make it perfect.
